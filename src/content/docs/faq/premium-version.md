Power Planner is mostly a free app, however for $4.99 USD, you can unlock a few additional features.

The purchase is a **one-time, lifetime purchase**, and it also syncs across your account, so if you purchase on Android, you'll also unlock premium on Windows and iOS, or vice versa!

The free version is limited to...

- 1 semester
- 5 grades per class
- 1 repeating bulk entry of a task/event
- 1 photo attachment per item
