If you're not receiving your password reset email, be sure to **check your junk mail**! The email should arrive within less than 5 minutes (typically a minute or less).

If you're still having trouble, please email support@powerplanner.net from your email associated with your Power Planner (so I can validate your ownership of the account), and I'll help you get your password reset!
