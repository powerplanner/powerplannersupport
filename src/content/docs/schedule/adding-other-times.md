---
title: Adding labs or other times for classes
description: If your class meets at a different time on one of the days of the week, like a lab class period, you can add different times in Power Planner.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

If your class meets at a different time on one of the days of the week, like a lab class period, simply click "+ add time" again for the class to add a different time entry for the class.

![Add time button](../../../assets/adding-other-times-1.png)

Then enter the different time schedule and save your changes!

![Adding other time](../../../assets/adding-other-times-2.png)

You now have a class that meets on different times of the week!

![Multiple times](../../../assets/adding-other-times-3.png)
