The iOS, Android, and Windows apps have a dark theme and support dark mode. Dark themes are enabled based on your OS settings. The apps will match whatever theme you have set for your computer or phone.

## Enabling dark mode on iOS

To enable the dark mode on iOS, see this article: [Use Dark Mode on your iPhone and iPad - Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108350)

## Enabling dark mode on Windows

To enable the dark mode on Windows, see this article: https://www.pcmag.com/how-to/how-to-enable-dark-mode-in-windows-10

## Enabling dark mode on Android

To enable the dark mode on Android, see this article: https://www.google.com/amp/s/9to5google.com/2018/12/17/android-dark-mode-theme-pie/amp/
